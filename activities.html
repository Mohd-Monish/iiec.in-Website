Regular Programs

Ongoing initiatives to build your entrepreneurial skills

Skill Development Workshops

Weekly hands-on workshops covering essential startup skills: pitch deck design, financial modeling, product development, digital marketing strategies, web development, and more.

Bi-weekly brainstorming and design thinking sessions where students pitch innovative startup ideas, receive mentorship feedback, and form teams to work on promising business concepts.

Connect with industry mentors, angel investors, and startup founders for guidance on your entrepreneurial journey. Get paired with experienced professionals in your domain.
