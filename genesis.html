What is GENESIS

GENESIS is the official orientation program of IIEC — the Innovation, Incubation & Entrepreneurship Cell at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University. It serves as the entry point for students who want to explore innovation, entrepreneurship, and skill development beyond the traditional classroom.

The event introduces IIEC's vision, team structure, upcoming initiatives, and the opportunities available for students across all departments. Whether you want to build a startup, develop technical skills, or contribute to campus growth — GENESIS is where it begins.

In collaboration with GeeksforGeeks — bringing additional learning resources and technical opportunities to attendees.
