Organization Overview

Who We Are

IIEC — Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell is the official student-led entrepreneurship hub of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University, Navi Mumbai. What started as a small group of passionate students at IIEC has grown into a nationally recognized movement — ranked Top 23 out of 4,000+ teams across India at the National Entrepreneurship Challenge (NEC) by E-Cell, IIT Bombay. We are one of the best entrepreneurship cells in Maharashtra.

We exist to bridge the gap between ideas and execution. Through skill development workshops, startup mentorship, national competitions, hackathons, coding bootcamps, and industry connections, IIEC provides the platform where raw ambition transforms into real ventures. We don't just study entrepreneurship — we practice it. From business plan competitions to startup incubation, our community of student founders is building the future.

Every member who joins IIEC becomes part of a legacy of innovation and entrepreneurship. We're building a culture where innovation is second nature, failures are celebrated as lessons, and every student at CSMU is empowered to think like a startup founder and leader.

Foundational Pillars

Mission & Vision

Guiding principles accelerating our ecosystem from university initiative to nationally celebrated startup accelerator.

Our Mission

To ignite the entrepreneurial spirit in every student at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University by providing hands-on startup experiences, real-world industry exposure, and a community that turns bold ideas into impactful ventures.

  • Foster innovation, design thinking, and creative problem-solving
  • Compete and excel at national-level entrepreneurship challenges like NEC IIT Bombay
  • Connect students with industry leaders, angel investors, and mentors
  • Build a launchpad for student-led startups and innovation projects

Our Vision

To establish IIEC as India's most recognized college entrepreneurship cell that produces successful student founders, award-winning teams, and a self-sustaining innovation ecosystem at CSMU, Navi Mumbai.

  • Grow a thriving community of student entrepreneurs and innovators
  • Incubate and launch student-led startups from CSMU campus
  • Achieve excellence at national competitions like NEC IIT Bombay and beyond
  • Build strategic partnerships with industry, incubators, and venture capital firms
Evolution & Milestones

Our Journey

Key milestones we've achieved from our grassroots founding to qualifying for national finals.

Dec 2024 Foundation

IIEC Founded

A group of passionate students recognized the need for an entrepreneurship platform at CSMU. IIEC was born with a vision to build startup culture from the ground up.

Dec 2024 – 2025 Ecosystem

Building Foundations

IIEC grew steadily — organizing events, workshops, and building a strong entrepreneurial community on campus.

2025 · National Finals Key Milestone

NEC — Top 23 All India

Achieved Top 23 rank out of 4,000+ teams at the National Entrepreneurship Challenge (NEC) by E-Cell, IIT Bombay — qualifying for the national finals and putting CSMU on the map.

2025 – 2026 Scaling

Expansion & Incubation

IIEC expanded scope to cover incubation, innovation labs, and national outreach.

Feb 2026 Flagship

GENESIS & Beyond

Successfully conducted GENESIS — our flagship orientation event in collaboration with GeeksforGeeks. Building the team, hosting events, and scaling our national presence.

Organizational DNA

Our Values

The guiding principles shaping every event, hackathon, and mentorship session at IIEC.

VALUE // 01

Innovation First

We encourage thinking differently, challenging norms, and finding creative startup solutions to real-world problems through design thinking and lean methodology.

VALUE // 02

Community Driven

Success is collective. Our student founder network builds each other up, shares knowledge through peer learning, and celebrates every startup milestone together.

VALUE // 03

Action Oriented

Ideas are great, execution is everything. We believe in learning by doing — through hackathons, startup bootcamps, and real project building — and failing forward fast.
