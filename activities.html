04 / The Founder's Journey

From First Spark to Venture.

How IIEC's interconnected activities guide students through every phase of building a viable startup.

Step 01

Ideate & Validate

Participate in Illuminate entrepreneurship workshops in collaboration with E-Cell IIT Bombay to discover high-value problems and validate market demand.

Step 02

Build & Prototype

Turn solutions into functional prototypes at TechAstra hackathons and Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026).

Step 03

Pitch & Refine

Pitch before ecosystem investors and industry mentors at Next Gen Pitch and the Innovation & Startup Expo.

Step 04

Incubate & Scale

Fast-track into IIEC incubation, access legal/seed support, and compete nationally at Eureka! E-Cell IIT Bombay.
