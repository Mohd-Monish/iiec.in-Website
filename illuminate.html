Syllabus Breakdown

What Will You Learn?

Four focused, industry-aligned learning modules crafted to build your entrepreneurial toolkit.

01

Business Models

Understand how businesses create, deliver, and capture value sustainably.

  • How modern businesses operate and survive
  • Identifying and segmenting customer demographics
  • Crafting compelling Value Propositions
  • Revenue models and pricing strategies
  • Hands-on Business Model Canvas (BMC) workshop
02

Finance Fundamentals

Develop a foundational understanding of startup cash flow, runway, and costs.

  • Demystifying business finances & unit economics
  • Analyzing revenue models vs. fixed & variable costs
  • Basic financial thinking and runway sustainability
  • Understanding profitability vs. top-line growth
  • Critical financial considerations for early founders
03

Startup Fundamentals

Understand the core principles required to validate and launch a viable venture.

  • Identifying market opportunities & pain points
  • Problem framing before jumping to solutions
  • Developing Minimum Viable Products (MVPs)
  • Customer discovery and iterative feedback loops
  • Strategic thinking from a founder's perspective
04

Entrepreneurial Mindset

Learn how entrepreneurs tackle complex problems, manage risk, and navigate ambiguity.

  • Cultivating a solution-oriented problem-solving mindset
  • Spotting opportunities in everyday inefficiencies
  • Structured frameworks for high-stakes decision making
  • Embracing calculated risk and learning from failure
  • Building lifelong entrepreneurial leadership skills
