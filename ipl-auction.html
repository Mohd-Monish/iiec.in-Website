Guidelines

Rules & Guidelines

Read the complete ruleset, game instructions, and scoring system to prepare for the auction.

General Instructions

  • Proper decorum must be maintained. Usage of any foul language will result in straight disqualification.
  • This is a group event.
  • Usage of any third-party applications that give you an unfair advantage will result in immediate disqualification and potential ban from future tournaments.
  • Each participating team is given 120 Crores as budget.
  • Players must bid for IPL franchises (10 total).
  • Failure to comply with limitations leads to immediate disqualification.
  • It is mandatory to form a squad of exactly 11 players.
  • Required squad composition: 1 Wicket-keeper, 4 Batters, 4 Bowlers, 2 All-rounders

Game Rules

  • Auction starts with bidding on IPL Franchises. Base price: 5 CR each. No team can go unsold; the last team sells at 15 CR.
  • One player from each team raises the baton and bids; the rest discuss internally.
  • Players bidding order is declared on the spot.
  • Blind bids occur during the game—teams privately pass chits with prices to auctioneers.
  • Winners of blind bids can block a team for the next player's bid.
  • Teams with more/less than 11 players or out of cash before team formation are disqualified.
