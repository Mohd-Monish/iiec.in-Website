About IIEC

Our mission, vision, and the journey ahead

Who We Are

IIEC - Innovation, Incubation & Entrepreneurship Cell (formerly E-Cell CSMU) is the official entrepreneurship hub of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University, Navi Mumbai. Founded in 2020, we are a student-led initiative dedicated to fostering innovation, nurturing entrepreneurial spirit, and creating a thriving startup ecosystem within our university.

We believe that every student has the potential to be an entrepreneur. Our role is to provide the platform, resources, and community that transforms ideas into ventures. From workshops and bootcamps to industry connects and mentorship programs, we're building the complete entrepreneurial journey for CSMU students.

As an evolving organization, we're not just building an institution – we're laying the foundation for generations of entrepreneurs to come. Every member who joins us becomes a pioneer, shaping the culture and direction of IIEC.

Our Mission

To inspire, educate, and empower the next generation of entrepreneurs at CSMU through hands-on experiences, industry exposure, and a supportive community that transforms innovative ideas into successful ventures.

  • Foster innovation and creative problem-solving
  • Provide skill development through workshops
  • Connect students with industry mentors
  • Create a platform for idea validation and growth

Our Vision

To become the leading student-run entrepreneurship cell in Maharashtra, producing successful founders, innovative ventures, and a self-sustaining ecosystem that puts CSMU on the national startup map.

  • Build a network of 1000+ student entrepreneurs
  • Launch 10 funded startups by 2027
  • Host regional-level startup events
  • Establish partnerships with incubators
