History of Growth

From a student initiative to a nationally recognized entrepreneurship powerhouse

2020

E-Cell Launch

Founded as E-Cell CSMU — a passionate student-led initiative to foster entrepreneurial mindset and build startup culture within the university.

2025

NEC — Top 23 All India

Ranked Top 23 out of 4,000+ teams at the National Entrepreneurship Challenge (NEC) by E-Cell, IIT Bombay. Made it to the national finals, earning recognition among the best in the country.

2025

Rebranded as IIEC

Evolved into IIEC — Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell. Expanded scope with incubation support, national outreach, and industry collaborations including GeeksforGeeks.

Current

Core Pillars

The four foundations that drive our mission forward

01

Incubation

Nurturing early-stage student startup ideas with mentorship, funding guidance, and a supportive incubation ecosystem to transform concepts into viable ventures and real businesses in college.

02

Innovation

Fostering a culture of creativity and experimentation through hackathons, coding competitions, tech fests, innovation labs, and hands-on problem-solving workshops for students.

03

Leadership

Developing future student leaders through hands-on experience, team management, community building, and real-world project execution at CSMU.

04

National Reach

Competing at the highest level — Top 23 at NEC, IIT Bombay out of 4,000+ teams. Connecting with India's national startup ecosystem through premier entrepreneurship competitions, industry partnerships, and innovation challenges.

What We Offer

Comprehensive support for every stage of your entrepreneurial journey

Workshops & Startup Bootcamps

Intensive training sessions covering business planning, product development, pitch deck design, financial modeling, marketing strategies, and the essential skills every student founder needs to succeed.

Mentorship & Industry Network

Access to a growing network of entrepreneurs, angel investors, industry experts, and experienced mentors who provide real guidance on your college startup journey.

Startup Launchpad

From ideation to launch — resources, venture capital connections, national-level exposure through NEC IIT Bombay, and end-to-end incubation support to turn your student startup vision into reality.
