Integrity & Quality

Brand Usage Guidelines

Please follow these rules to maintain visual consistency and brand recognition across all platforms.

✓

Recommended Use (Do's)

  • Maintain Clear Space: Ensure minimum padding equal to 20% of logo width around all edges.
  • High-Contrast Backdrops: Place over deep dark backgrounds (`#09090b`, `#18181b`) or clean, light surfaces.
  • Keep Proportions: Always lock aspect ratio (1:1) when scaling to avoid distortion.
  • Use Vector SVG: Prefer `.svg` for digital headers and print posters for maximum sharpness.
✕

Prohibited Use (Don'ts)

  • Do NOT stretch or skew: Never distort the horizontal or vertical scale of the mark.
  • Do NOT alter brand colors: Never recolor the mark with unauthorized hues or unnatural gradients.
  • Do NOT place on busy photos: Never overlay on cluttered imagery without a dark scrim backdrop.
  • Do NOT rotate off-axis: Keep the orientation upright at all times.

Press Kit & Boilerplate

Verified descriptions of IIEC for event press releases, sponsor brochures, speaker bios, and media publications.

Short Description (50 Words)

IIEC (Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell) is the premier student-led startup ecosystem at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University (CSMU), Navi Mumbai. Ranked Top 23 All India at NEC IIT Bombay, IIEC empowers campus founders through incubation, hackathons, and venture launchpads under the motto "Where Vision Drives Venture."

Standard Full Boilerplate (120 Words)

The Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell (IIEC) at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University (CSMU), Navi Mumbai, is a dynamic student-led organization established in December 2024 to cultivate a high-impact startup ecosystem in Maharashtra. Driven by the mission "Where Vision Drives Venture," IIEC bridges the critical leap from campus concepts to scalable enterprises. The cell achieved national acclaim by ranking Top 23 out of 4,000+ colleges across India at the National Entrepreneurship Challenge (NEC), hosted by E-Cell IIT Bombay. IIEC organizes flagship initiatives including TechAstra, GENESIS, pitch competitions, and startup bootcamps in collaboration with industry pioneers.
