01 / Start here

What if the problem you've been ignoring is actually an opportunity?

UNLOCK gives students a practical and interactive space to explore entrepreneurial thinking, innovation, ideation and problem-solving.

Discovering→ Questioning→ Exploring→ Thinking→ Ideating

02 / What you'll explore

Four directions.
No fixed answers.

An interactive session built around four ways of thinking — each one something you already do, just not on purpose yet.

01

Entrepreneurial Thinking

Understand how entrepreneurs look at problems, opportunities and possibilities.

02

Ideation

Explore how ideas emerge from observations and questions.

03

Problem-Solving

Look at everyday problems differently and think beyond obvious solutions.

04

Collaboration

Exchange perspectives and build on other students' thinking.

03 / The experience

Come with questions. Leave with possibilities.

The session moves through four simple acts. No stages, no spotlights — just a way of looking.

ACT 01 NOTICE.

Notice the problems around you.

↓
ACT 02 QUESTION.

Question why they exist.

↓
ACT 03 EXPLORE.

Explore what could change.

↓
ACT 04 CONNECT.

Connect ideas, people and possibilities.
