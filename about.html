About IIEC

Our mission, vision, and the journey ahead

Who We Are

IIEC — Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell is the official student-led entrepreneurship hub of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University. What started as a small group of passionate students has grown into a nationally recognized movement — ranked Top 23 out of 4,000+ teams across India at the National Entrepreneurship Challenge (NEC) by E-Cell, IIT Bombay.

We exist to bridge the gap between ideas and execution. Through workshops, mentorship, national competitions, hackathons, and industry connections, IIEC provides the platform where raw ambition transforms into real ventures. We don't just study entrepreneurship — we practice it.

Every member who joins IIEC becomes part of a legacy. We're building a culture where innovation is second nature, failures are celebrated as lessons, and every student is empowered to think like a founder.

Our Mission

To ignite the entrepreneurial spirit in every student by providing hands-on experiences, real-world exposure, and a community that turns bold ideas into impactful ventures.

  • Foster innovation and creative problem-solving
  • Compete and excel at national-level challenges
  • Connect students with industry leaders and mentors
  • Build a launchpad for student-led startups

Our Vision

To establish IIEC as a nationally recognized entrepreneurship cell that produces successful founders, award-winning teams, and a self-sustaining innovation ecosystem.

  • Grow a thriving community of student entrepreneurs
  • Incubate and launch student-led startups
  • Achieve excellence at national competitions like NEC
  • Build strategic partnerships with industry and incubators

Our Journey

Milestones we've achieved and the road ahead

2020

E-Cell Founded

A group of passionate students recognized the need for an entrepreneurship platform at CSMU. E-Cell CSMU was born with a vision to build startup culture from the ground up.

2020–2024

Building Foundations

E-Cell CSMU grew steadily — organizing events, workshops, and building a strong entrepreneurial community on campus.

2025

NEC — Top 23 All India

Achieved Top 23 rank out of 4,000+ teams at the National Entrepreneurship Challenge (NEC) by E-Cell, IIT Bombay — qualifying for the national finals and putting CSMU on the map.

2025

Evolution to IIEC

E-Cell CSMU transforms into IIEC — Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell, expanding scope to cover incubation, innovation labs, and national outreach.
