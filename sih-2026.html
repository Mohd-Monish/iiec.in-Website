Smart India Hackathon 2026

CSMU – SIH 2026 Internal Hackathon
Student Team Registration

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University (CSMU) is organizing the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026 – Internal Hackathon on 31st August & 1st September 2026. Submit innovative solutions to real-world problems.

Event Dates: 31st Aug & 1st Sept 2026 | Official SIH Portal: sih.gov.in

Team Structure

Each team must have 6 students (including Leader).

Female Member Mandate

At least 1 female student is required.

Eligibility

All members must be full-time students of CSMU.

Live Demo

Shortlisted teams present live demos before evaluation panel.
