What is GENESIS

GENESIS is the official orientation program of IIEC — the Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University, Navi Mumbai. It serves as the entry point for students across all departments who want to explore innovation, entrepreneurship, startup development, and skill building beyond the traditional classroom.

The event introduces IIEC's vision, team structure, upcoming initiatives like TechAstra and NEC IIT Bombay, and the startup opportunities available for every student. Whether you want to build a tech startup, develop coding skills, learn digital marketing, or contribute to campus growth — GENESIS is where your entrepreneurial journey begins.

In collaboration with GeeksforGeeks — bringing additional learning resources and technical opportunities to attendees.

Why Attend GENESIS

What you take away from this event

Understand IIEC's Startup Roadmap

Learn what IIEC does, its innovation and entrepreneurship plans for 2026, and how you can be part of the startup ecosystem at CSMU.

Learn Beyond Classrooms

Discover startup skills, coding workshops, and real-world experiences that go beyond your academic curriculum and prepare you for the industry.

Real-World Startup Skill Exposure

Get introduced to lean startup thinking, product development, pitch deck building, design thinking, and professional skills essential for every student founder.

Know How to Join IIEC

Understand the recruitment process, team roles across 7 portfolios, campus entrepreneurship opportunities, and how to apply to be part of the innovation cell.

GeeksforGeeks Learning Opportunities

Access exclusive coding courses, data structures & algorithms resources, web development paths, and tech community opportunities through GfG collaboration.
