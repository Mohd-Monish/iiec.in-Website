04 / How to register

Three steps. Then it's your turn.

Complete your Eureka! registration first. Then submit your details to IIEC.

01

Register on Eureka!

Head to ecell.in/eureka and register your startup before submitting the form below. You can skip the detailed startup information for now if you prefer.

02

Use the NEC referral

Select NEC under “How did you hear about Eureka?” and enter NEC2661839 in the Referral Code / NEC ID field.

03

Submit your team

You'll receive a Eureka! Team ID by email. Share it with IIEC along with your team details through the form below.

!
The referral code is mandatory.

Without NEC2661839 in the referral field we can't track your status, you won't receive extra points, and your pitch will not be evaluated at the Pre-Zonal level.

06 / FAQ

Still deciding?

Anything else, write to us and the IIEC team will get back to you.

Do I need a registered startup to participate?

No. An idea is enough. Students, aspiring entrepreneurs, and early-stage startups are all welcome to pitch.

Is the Eureka! registration really compulsory?

Yes. Without a Eureka! 2026 registration carrying our NEC ID NEC2661839, IIT Bombay cannot evaluate your pitch at the Pre-Zonal level.

When do I submit my pitch deck?

Not with this form. After your registration is confirmed, we'll send a separate form to collect your startup details and pitch deck link.

Can students from outside CSMU take part?

Yes. Local startups, aspiring entrepreneurs, and outside participants are all invited to pitch.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. All participants receive recognition and certificates, and top teams stand a chance to be fast-tracked to the Eureka! 2026 Zonal Rounds.

Who do I contact for help?

Email ecell-student-rep@csmu.ac.in with your question and team name.
