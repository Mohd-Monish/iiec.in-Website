History of Growth

From a student initiative to a university-backed innovation powerhouse

2020

E-Cell Launch

Founded as E-Cell CSMU — a passionate student initiative to foster entrepreneurial mindset and startup culture within the university.

2023

NEC @ IIT Bombay

Represented at the prestigious National Entrepreneurship Challenge, gaining national recognition and invaluable connections with India's top innovators.

2025

Rebranded as IIEC

Evolved into IIEC — Innovation, Incubation & Entrepreneurship Cell. Now university-backed with expanded scope covering incubation, innovation labs, and national outreach.
