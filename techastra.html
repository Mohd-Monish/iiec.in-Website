What is TechAstra?

TechAstra is the largest annual technical fest of CSMU — a celebration of technology, innovation, and creativity. From hackathons and coding battles to robotics, tech quizzes, and workshops — TechAstra brings together the brightest minds on campus for one power-packed day.

Organized by IIEC (Incubation, Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell), TechAstra is designed to push boundaries, spark ideas, and give students a platform to showcase their technical brilliance.

This year, we're going bigger than ever. And we need YOU to make it happen.
